🔒 Privacy & what we store

On this device: the name you type, your scores, streaks and quiz history are saved in your browser only. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Sent to the teacher: when a question is answered we also send the name, the score, the device type and the network (IP) address and approximate city to the teacher's private spreadsheet. This is used only to see how the class is practising.

Never collected: we do not ask for an email, phone number, photo or address, and we show no adverts.

Parents: this app is intended to be used with your knowledge. To remove stored progress, tap ⭐ in the header and choose “Start Again”.
